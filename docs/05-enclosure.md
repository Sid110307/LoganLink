# 05. Enclosure

The dongle sits straight out of the OBD-II socket: plug nose into the car, board flat behind it. 57.8 x 45.8 mm, 27.6 mm out
from the socket face. Short and square to the socket, so a knee or a glovebox lid pushes it straight in, not sideways.

Everything is generated by `mechanical/dongle_freecad.py`. Run it in FreeCAD (Macro > Execute, or through the MCP bridge). It
writes `LoganLink-dongle.FCStd`, `dongle.step` and the STL files next to itself. All sizes are in the `Params` sheet and the
`P` dict at the top of the script.

## Parts

| Part | File | Print |
|------|------|-------|
| Front shell | `dongle-front.stl` | Face down, no supports |
| Retainer | `dongle-retainer.stl` | Plate down, no supports |
| Rear shell | `dongle-rear.stl` | Roof down (already flipped), no supports |
| Light pipe | `dongle-pipe.stl` | Clear PETG, upright |
| Screws | 4 x M2 x 18 pan head self-tapping for plastic | |

Material: ASA or PETG. A closed car in the sun gets the glovebox past 70 °C, so not PLA. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 30 % infill.

## Stack (from the car outwards)

| z (mm) | Part |
|--------|------|
| 0 to 2 | Front shell face. The plug nose passes through it |
| 2 to 5 | Plug flange, sitting on the inside of the face and on the four boss stubs |
| 5 to 7 | Retainer plate, clamping the flange |
| 5 to 13 | Plug body behind the flange, through the retainer opening |
| 7 to 18 | Retainer corner posts |
| 13 to 18 | Wire space, crimp pins to board |
| 18 to 19.6 | PCB |
| 19.6 to 26.1 | Parts (6 mm max) |
| 26.1 to 27.6 | Rear shell roof |

Plugging in pushes the plug back into the retainer, which carries the load through the posts and screws into the front shell.
Pulling out pulls the flange against the front face. Neither load goes through the board or the wires.

## Assembly

1. Cut six 25 mm lengths of 22 AWG wire. Solder them into J1 from the bottom of the board.
2. Crimp a 57964-9702 pin on each free end. Insert into the housing: J1.1 to cavity 16, J1.2 to 4, J1.3 to 5, J1.4 to 7.
   J1.5 and J1.6 (cavities 6 and 14) only if CAN is fitted.
3. Push the plug nose out through the front shell face from inside, flange against the face.
4. Drop the retainer over the plug body onto the flange.
5. Lay the board on the retainer posts and drive the four screws through the board and posts into the front shell bosses.
   Snug, not tight.
6. Press the light pipe into the rear shell from inside.
7. Snap the rear shell onto the front shell. The lip has a groove on each long side that catches a bump on the front shell.

To flash over USB, pop the rear shell off. Normal updates go over the air.

## Fit check

Before printing all of it, print the front shell and retainer only and measure the real Molex housing:

| Param | Default | Measure |
|-------|---------|---------|
| `plug_w`, `plug_w_bot`, `plug_h` | 39, 33, 17 | Nose trapezoid, top width, bottom width, height |
| `flange_t`, `flange_ext` | 3, 1.2 | Flange thickness and overhang past the nose |
| `plug_body` | 8 | Body depth behind the flange |
| `gap` | 0.3 | Increase if the plug is tight in the face opening |

Change the numbers, rerun the script, check the screws reach the bosses (`pcb z` in the output).
